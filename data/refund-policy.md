# ShopSmart Refund Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** June 1, 2026

---

## 1. Return Window

- All standard products must be returned within **14 days** of the delivery date.
- Requests submitted after 14 days from delivery are automatically ineligible for a refund.

## 2. Product Condition

- The product **must not have been used** to qualify for a refund.
- Items that show signs of use, wear, or damage caused by the customer are not eligible.
- Original packaging is not required but recommended.

## 3. Electronics Policy

- Electronics that have been **activated or registered** cannot be refunded, regardless of purchase date.
- Unactivated electronics follow the standard 14-day return window.
- Software licenses, digital downloads, and SaaS subscriptions are non-refundable once accessed.

## 4. Gift Cards

- **Gift cards are strictly non-refundable** under any circumstances.
- This applies to all denominations and both physical and digital gift cards.

## 5. Custom and Personalized Products

- **Custom-made or personalized products cannot be refunded.**
- This includes engraved items, monogrammed products, made-to-order furniture, and tailor-made clothing.

## 6. High-Value Refunds

- Refunds for orders **above $500** require **manager approval** before processing.
- The standard processing time for manager-approved refunds is 3–5 business days.

## 7. Refund Processing

- Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within **5–7 business days**.
- Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to a defect or error on our part.

## 8. Damaged or Defective Items

- Items that arrive damaged or defective are eligible for a full refund or replacement within **30 days**.
- Photo evidence of damage may be required.

## 9. Exceptions

- Items marked as **Final Sale** are not eligible for returns or refunds.
- Perishable goods, hazardous materials, and intimate apparel cannot be returned.
